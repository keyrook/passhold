Passhold is a password manager, free forever, that autofills your logins, keeps your passkeys and generates strong passwords. Your vault is end-to-end encrypted on your device and syncs across your computers with a Keyrook account. It is open source, so anyone can check how it works.

AUTOFILL ON EVERY SITE
• One click in a sign-in field fills the right account for that site.
• Saves new logins as you sign in, and updates the ones you change.
• Fills cards and addresses at checkout, only where you choose.
• Signs you in with passkeys on the sites that offer them.
• Works in forms inside frames, as many bank and shop sign-ins are built.
• A keyboard shortcut opens your vault at any time.

STRONG AND UNIQUE
• Password generator: a strong, unique one for every account.
• Choose the length and characters, and leave out look-alikes such as 0 and O.
• Security checkup: finds weak and reused ones, and shows what to fix first.

SYNC ACROSS YOUR COMPUTERS
• One Keyrook account, with its own password or with Google or GitHub.
• Unlock with Touch ID, Windows Hello, a PIN or a security key.
• Encrypted backups you keep yourself.

EASY TO MOVE IN, EASY TO LEAVE
• Import your logins with a CSV file, from your browser or most other managers.
• Export them at any time, as a CSV file or an encrypted backup. Your data stays yours.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Zero-knowledge: everything is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device before it leaves. Keyrook stores what you sync and cannot read it.
• The key that opens your vault never reaches Keyrook.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
• Site icons are built in, so no server learns where you have accounts.
• An account is offered only on its own site, so a look-alike page gets nothing.
• Fields you cannot see are never filled, and cards are filled only on secure pages.
• A copied password is cleared from the clipboard after a short while.
• Open source: read the code on GitHub.

FREE FOREVER
• Everything above is free, for good, for everyone. Paid plans add extras on top, and nothing here will ever move behind them.

ALSO IN YOUR VAULT
• Payment cards, addresses and secure notes, in folders, with custom fields.
• A trash that keeps what you delete, and every earlier version of a login.
• Locks itself when you step away.
